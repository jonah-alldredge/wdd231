Membership Levels

Non-Profit Membership Level

Non-Profit Membership Level

  • Access to general chamber networking events
  • Basic organization listing in the community directory
  • Posting privileges on the public community bulletin board
  • Cost: Free ($0/month)

Bronze Membership Level

Bronze Membership Level

  • Includes all Non-Profit level benefits
  • Standard business directory listing with direct website link
  • Access to member-to-member discount and referral programs
  • 10% discount on paid chamber workshop tickets
  • Cost: $10/month

Silver Membership Level

Silver Membership Level

  • Includes all Bronze level benefits
  • Enhanced directory listing featuring your company logo and description
  • Quarterly social media promotional shout-outs and newsletter features
  • 15% discount on all paid chamber events and advertising spaces
  • Invitation to exclusive quarterly business growth workshops
  • Cost: $20/month

Gold Membership Level

Gold Membership Level

  • Includes all Silver level benefits
  • Premium homepage spotlight positioning and featured directory ranking
  • Priority sponsorship opportunities for annual summits and expos
  • 25% discount and complimentary VIP seating at all paid events
  • Direct access to monthly executive leadership roundtables
  • Cost: $30/month
